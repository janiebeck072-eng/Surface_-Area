Formula dock

Prisms: LSA = perimeter of base × height; TSA = LSA + 2(base area).

Cylinders: LSA = 2πrh; TSA = 2πrh + 2πr².

Regular pyramids: LSA = ½(perimeter of base)(slant height); TSA = LSA + base area.

Cones: LSA = πrℓ; TSA = πrℓ + πr².

Composite figures: Add exposed areas only; do not count hidden shared faces.

Use π = 3.14 for every circular problem. Units of surface area are square units.
